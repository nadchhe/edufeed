Delete your EduFeed account

In the app (recommended)

  1. Open EduFeed and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email YOUR-SUPPORT-EMAIL from the address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your profile, posts and media, comments, likes, saved posts, messages, followers and following, preferences, and your login. Backups are overwritten within a short period.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
