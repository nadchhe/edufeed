EduFeed Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

EduFeed ("we", "us") is operated by YOUR-NAME-OR-COMPANY, YOUR-COUNTRY. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Contact: YOUR-SUPPORT-EMAIL.

1. What we collect

  • Account data: email address, username, profile photo and bio. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email and profile photo from Google.
  • Content you create: posts, photos, videos, comments, likes, follows, saved posts and direct messages.
  • Usage data: posts you open, your daily view count and knowledge points, and notifications.
  • Purchase status: whether you have an active EduFeed Pro subscription. Payments are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your card details.
  • Reports: reports you submit about content, and reports others submit about yours.
  • Technical data: basic device and connection information handled by our service providers to run and secure the app.

2. How we use it

To provide the app (accounts, feed, messaging, notifications), to apply the free daily viewing limit and unlock Pro, to keep the community safe (reviewing reports, removing content and accounts that break our Terms), to fix problems, and to answer your questions. We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

Only service providers that run the app for us:

  • Google Firebase (sign-in and authentication)
  • Supabase (database and file storage)
  • Cloudflare (hosting of our server)
  • RevenueCat (subscription management)
  • Apple and Google (app stores and payments)

Your public profile, posts and comments are visible to other users. Direct messages are visible only to the people in the conversation. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect users' safety.

4. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings → Delete Account). This permanently deletes your profile, posts, media, comments, likes, messages, followers and preferences, and your login. Some copies may remain in backups for a short period before they are overwritten. Subscriptions are not cancelled automatically; cancel them in the App Store or Google Play. See also how to delete your account.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to certain processing. You can edit your profile in the app, delete your account in the app, or email YOUR-SUPPORT-EMAIL and we will respond within 30 days.

6. Security

We use encrypted connections and access controls, but no system is perfectly secure.

7. Children

EduFeed is not for children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country, if higher). If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
